Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

HAPI Automation is used by one person, its owner, with their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the Google data the owner authorizes (for example Google Tasks), and only to sync it with the owner's own notes.

  • Storage: nothing is stored on this website. This site holds only these information pages.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
